## U - underscores

Generally speaking, it is an excellent hyper pop album. Many people might be overwhelmed by hyper pop as a whole, but I would say generally 
underscores is successful at creating an accessible work. The melodies on the album are great. Sonically very good, some slightly overwhelming 
moments in Tell Me (U Want It), and Music, but a lot of the slower moving tracks in the back half I really appreciate.


## Music Review

The Peace is awesome! Super melodramatic, but fits the vision for what this album is going for.

## Final Rating
I would liken this to a beatbox, or something to be consumed at an edgy party. I don't quite know. I want to shoot for something that feels more edgy,
but no drink immediately fits my vision for this. Beatbox is a satisfactory description for now at least.
